# CRT Modding: adding RGB input support
## for CRT that only have **composite** input

Yep, you're deep down the rabbit hole.
You got yourself a CRT TV and most likely an HDMI to RCA converter, and the image looks... garbage.  
Not only cheap converters do not deliver a clear image, having a composite input does not help.  
That's why RGB input is the way: some TVs do officially support it and some don't;
in my case the SCART input on the back of the TV only supports composite, 
that's why we're adding RGB support by modding the internal *jungle chip* inside the TV.
